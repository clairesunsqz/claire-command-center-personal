Claire Command Center Personal

A private, single-user personal assistant integration that uses read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar to support personal briefings and schedule awareness.

Read-only by design.

The application cannot send or modify email and cannot create, edit, or delete calendar events.

Purpose

This application is operated solely for the account owner's personal use. It helps summarize relevant personal email and upcoming calendar information while keeping the user in control of all consequential actions.

Access

  • Gmail: read-only access.
  • Google Calendar: read-only access.
  • No advertising, resale, or access for unrelated third parties.
